Delete Your Vatexs Account

Last updated: September 1, 2026

You can permanently delete your Vatexs account and personal data at any time, either directly in the app or by requesting it here.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Vatexs app and sign in
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Scroll to the Danger zone section
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm

Your account is deleted immediately. If you have an order still in progress (payment held in escrow), you'll need to complete or resolve it first before the app will let you delete your account.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, email support@vatexs.store from the address associated with your Vatexs account and ask us to delete it. We'll verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, photo, phone, location, bio)
  • Your listings and listing photos
  • Your messages and conversations
  • Your favorites and saved payout bank details

What we keep, and why

If you've ever bought or sold something on Vatexs, we retain your order and transaction records (amounts, dates, status) even after your account is deleted. This is required for financial record-keeping, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes, and to resolve any disputes tied to past orders. These retained records are disassociated from your name — your profile is anonymized to "Deleted user" and your login is permanently disabled, so the data can no longer be used to identify or contact you.

See our Privacy Policy for more detail on how we handle your data.
